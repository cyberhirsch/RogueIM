RogueIM

The ICQ feeling, without servers, without a phone number, end-to-end encrypted.

  • A buddy list docked to the edge of your screen, with status, away messages and the sound of an incoming message.
  • Peer to peer: no account on anyone's server. Offline messages wait encrypted in public Nostr mailboxes.
  • Voice calls and screen sharing, files over a direct connection, several devices on one account.
  • Windows, macOS and Linux. Free software (GPL-3.0). Alpha.
